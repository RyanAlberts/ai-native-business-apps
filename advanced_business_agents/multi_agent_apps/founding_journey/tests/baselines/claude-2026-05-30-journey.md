## ➡️ What's Next (Days 30–90)

1. **Get a 409A valuation before issuing any employee stock options.** A 409A establishes the fair market value of common stock and sets the exercise price for ISO grants. Issuing options without a 409A exposes the company to IRS §409A penalties. As soon as you close seed funding (or before if you hire early), engage a 409A provider (Carta, Pulley, or Andersen Tax) — typical cost $1,500–$3,000 pre-seed.

2. **Set up Carta for cap table management.** Carta integrates with Mercury and tracks Ada's and Ben's restricted stock, vesting schedules, and future option grants in one place. Investors will expect a clean, current Carta cap table during seed diligence. Set it up now while the cap table is simple (two founders), not during a funding scramble.

3. **Engage a robotics/manufacturing-specialist insurance broker and bind all policies before any pilot.** D&O must be bound 30–60 days before seed close. GL + Product Liability must be in hand before the first robot crosses a customer's dock — treat COI readiness as a sales prerequisite, not a post-revenue task. Enterprise 3PLs commonly require $5M per occurrence; verify your first customer contract's requirements before binding limits.

4. **Draft customer-facing commercial agreements before signing any LOIs or pilots.** Your attorney should prepare: (a) a Pilot/Evaluation Agreement with IP protection, indemnification, and warranty limitations; (b) a Master Service Agreement / Sales Agreement for production deployments. For a hardware company with robots in third-party warehouses, poorly drafted contracts are the second-largest liability exposure after an uninsured incident.

5. **File provisional patent application(s) for core robotics IP.** If Ada or Ben have developed novel picking algorithms, sensing approaches, or mechanical innovations, a provisional patent application (~$3,000–$5,000 with a patent attorney) establishes a priority date, buys 12 months of "patent pending" status, and is a meaningful signal in seed-round diligence for a deep-tech hardware company. Engage a patent attorney who specializes in robotics or mechanical systems.
