## Description

Starting a company means a month of back-office paperwork: incorporation,
EIN, the 83(b) election, an operating agreement, a bank account, sales-tax
nexus, a compliance calendar. Stripe Atlas does a slice of it for $500, in a
walled garden, DE-C-Corp-only.

Keel is the open-source alternative. Its flagship **Founding Journey** takes
your company profile once and runs five specialist AI agents in the right
order — incorporation → 83(b) → legal docs → banking → compliance — then
hands you a complete **Day-0 Formation Packet**: a printable PDF, the actual
documents, and a real calendar file of your deadlines.

It runs on the Claude subscription you already pay for (no API key), it's
"prepare-to-submit" so a human always reviews before filing, and it's
Apache-2.0 — fork it, extend it, run it for your whole accelerator batch.

**Why makers care:**
- 🆓 $0 instead of $500 — runs on your existing Claude sub
- 📎 Hands you real files, not advice (PDF packet, 83(b) letter, `.ics`)
- 🧩 12+ agents; add your own by copying a folder
- 🔓 Apache-2.0, self-hosted, zero telemetry
