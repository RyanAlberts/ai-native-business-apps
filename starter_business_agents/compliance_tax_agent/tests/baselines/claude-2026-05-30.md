## Recommendation Summary

Three things need your attention **right now**, in this order. First, **pay Delaware's $300 LLC franchise tax at [corp.delaware.gov/paytaxes](https://corp.delaware.gov/paytaxes/) before June 1** — if your LLC was formed in any prior year, you have roughly 48 hours before late fees accrue and Delaware begins the dissolution clock. Second, **register as a foreign LLC in California and start paying the $800/yr minimum franchise tax** — your 3PL warehouse gives you physical presence in California the moment inventory hits that warehouse floor, and every month you delay accrues back liability to the FTB. Third, **register with California's CDTFA for a sales tax permit immediately** — that same physical nexus obligates you to collect and remit sales tax on all California-destination Shopify orders today. Amazon handles its own remittance on FBA sales via marketplace-facilitator rules, but your Shopify channel is entirely your responsibility. Everything else in this plan builds off those three actions.

## Sales Tax Nexus Analysis

### Physical Nexus States

| State | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **California** | CA 3PL warehouse — inventory on the floor = physical presence | Register with CDTFA; collect on all Shopify CA-destination sales; file quarterly |
| **States with Amazon FBA warehouses** | Amazon stores your inventory in fulfillment centers across ~20+ states (TX, NJ, PA, WA, FL, KY, TN, IN, AZ, NV, OH, GA, and others) | See Marketplace Facilitator section below |

> **Note on the 3PL location:** If your CA 3PL is the only non-Amazon warehouse, California is your sole physical-nexus state requiring *your* direct registration. Confirm whether your 3PL is CA only — if they have facilities in other states and store your inventory there, you may have additional physical nexus. Ask your 3PL for a "state footprint" letter.

### Economic Nexus States

With ~$200k total year-1 revenue across Shopify + Amazon, your **economic nexus exposure depends almost entirely on how much is Shopify direct** (Amazon FBA sales are collected/remitted by Amazon and in most states don't count toward *your* thresholds). Typical breakdowns to watch:

| Scenario | Exposure |
|---|---|
| Shopify = $50k or less | Very unlikely to cross any state threshold in year 1 |
| Shopify = $100k–$150k | Possible threshold crossing in 1–3 high-volume states |
| Shopify = $150k+ | Run a full nexus study — multiple state registrations likely needed |

**Most states** use a threshold of **$100,000 in sales OR 200 transactions** in the prior or current calendar year — but exact rules vary and change. Notable exceptions to verify with your CPA:
- **Texas:** $500,000 threshold (higher bar)
- **California:** $500,000 threshold for economic nexus — but moot for you since you already have *physical* nexus
- **Kansas:** Previously no threshold (taxed from dollar one) — verify current rule
- **Missouri, Florida, Mississippi:** Have had unique threshold structures; verify current year

> ⚠️ **Don't rely on these thresholds as settled fact** — states update them. Use [TaxJar's nexus tracker](https://www.taxjar.com/state-sales-tax-nexus/) or Avalara's nexus tool to monitor your Shopify channel state-by-state as revenue grows.

### Marketplace Facilitator States

**Amazon FBA — Largely Covered.** Every US state with a sales tax has marketplace facilitator (MF) laws as of 2026. Under these laws, Amazon collects and remits sales tax on your FBA sales in all applicable states. **You do not register in or remit to those states for Amazon channel revenue.**

However — two important asterisks:
1. **FBA physical nexus still exists.** Your inventory sits in Amazon warehouses in ~20+ states, creating physical nexus. Most MF states don't require seller registration when the MF handles collection, but a handful of states have historically pursued FBA sellers for state *income tax* nexus (franchise/business activity). This is an evolving area — ask your CPA whether states like Texas, Pennsylvania, or New Jersey require a foreign-entity registration for income tax purposes given FBA inventory.
2. **Shopify is NOT a marketplace facilitator** for your direct store. Shopify provides a payment processor; the legal seller of record is you. Every Shopify sale is your nexus exposure.

|---|---|---|

| All other states (Shopify) | Run nexus analysis; register in any state where Shopify sales exceed threshold | Within 60 days |

## State Filings (Annual)

### Delaware
- **State income tax:** Delaware does not tax LLC income earned *outside* Delaware. As a pass-through, income flows to your personal return (CA resident income taxes apply instead).
- **[Annual LLC Franchise Tax](https://corp.delaware.gov/paytaxes/)** — flat **$300/yr**, due **June 1** — ⚠️ due in 2 days
- No DE sales tax (Delaware has no state sales tax).
- *State note:* Delaware is excellent for formation paperwork and venture fundraising due to Court of Chancery case law, but for a bootstrapped e-commerce LLC it adds ~$300/yr and a registered-agent fee with no operational benefit. Confirm with your CPA whether re-domiciling to California makes sense once you're established.

### California
- **State income/franchise tax (FTB Form 568):** LLC Return of Income + **$800 minimum franchise tax**, due April 15 annually (15th day of 4th month after tax year end) — [ftb.ca.gov](https://www.ftb.ca.gov/)
- **[Statement of Information](https://www.sos.ca.gov/business-programs/business-entities/statements)** — approximately **$20**, due biennially — verify your specific due date based on CA registration date
- **CA sales tax returns (CDTFA):** At ~$200k revenue, CDTFA will likely assign **quarterly filing**:
  - Q1 (Jan–Mar): due ~April 30
  - Q2 (Apr–Jun): due ~July 31
  - Q3 (Jul–Sep): due ~October 31
  - Q4 (Oct–Dec): due ~January 31
  Verify your assigned filing frequency in your CDTFA account after registration.
- *State note from tool:* California's **$800 minimum franchise tax applies to all LLCs regardless of revenue** — even a $0-revenue LLC owes it. The Statement of Information is due within 90 days of initial CA registration and biennially thereafter. Missing the SOI triggers a $250 penalty automatically.

## Bookkeeping Setup

**Recommended tool: QuickBooks Online (QBO)** — specifically the Simple Start or Essentials tier (~$30–$65/mo). Reason: every e-commerce CPA in the US can open your QBO file without an export/import step. Shopify has a native QBO integration; for Amazon FBA, connect **A2X** ($19–$49/mo) as middleware — A2X summarizes Amazon settlements into clean journal entries and saves 3–5 hours/month of reconciliation work. This combo is the de-facto standard for Shopify + Amazon FBA brands.

**Supporting practices:**
- **Separate business bank account and credit card from day 1** — commingling is the single fastest way to lose LLC liability protection
- **Reconcile monthly**, not at year-end — Amazon settlements + Shopify payouts hit at different cadences; monthly reconciliation keeps you sane and catches errors early
- **Collect a W-9 before paying any contractor $600+** — request it with the first invoice, not in January when everyone ignores emails
- **Track COGS meticulously** — for a product brand, cost of goods sold directly offsets revenue; poor COGS tracking is the #1 reason e-commerce founders overpay taxes
- **Separate Amazon FBA inventory costs** — use a dedicated "FBA Inventory" asset account so you can value ending inventory for Schedule C / Form 1065 accurately
- **Track sales-tax collected as a liability** (not revenue) — CDTFA tax you collect is not your income; it sits in a "Sales Tax Payable" account until you remit

## Common Pitfalls

- **Not registering as a foreign LLC in California before generating revenue there.** The FTB assesses the $800 minimum franchise tax retroactively from the date you first "do business" in CA — which for you started the day inventory arrived at the 3PL. Every month you delay is another month of potential back-tax exposure.
- **Assuming Amazon FBA nexus is fully handled by marketplace facilitator rules.** Amazon remits *sales tax* for you in MF states, but several states may still assert franchise/income tax nexus based on FBA inventory. This is unsettled law in some states — get explicit CPA guidance before ignoring it.
- **Missing estimated quarterly federal taxes.** As a pass-through owner with no W-2 withholding, you owe quarterly estimated payments. Skipping even one triggers IRS underpayment penalties. **Q2 is due June 16 — 17 days from today.** Set aside ~25–30% of net profit quarterly.
- **Not collecting W-9s from contractors upfront.** Chasing contractors for W-9s in January (1099 season) is painful and sometimes impossible if they've moved on. Make it a condition of first payment.
- **Thinking the FinCEN BOI filing is still required for your LLC.** It isn't — US-formed entities are exempt under the 2025 interim final rule. Ignoring this exemption just wastes time; the real risk is the opposite: if someone incorrectly tells you the old rule still applies and you file unnecessarily. Check the [FinCEN BOI FAQ](https://www.fincen.gov/boi-faqs) for current guidance.
- **Skincare-specific: not tracking ingredient/labeling compliance costs as business expenses.** FDA cosmetics labeling requirements (MOCRA 2022) have created new compliance costs for skincare brands. These are deductible business expenses — document them properly.
